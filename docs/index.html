Monthly Summary

China's total electricity demand reached 1,038.5 TWh in July 2026, up 2.9 percent year-on-year, driven by extended heat waves and sustained industrial output. Clean energy sources — nuclear, hydro, wind, solar, and bioenergy — covered 44.8 percent of generation, 3.5 percentage points above July 2025 and the highest clean-energy share for a summer month on record. Crude oil imports fell sharply to 35.7 Mt (−24.1 percent YoY) as refinery maintenance reduced throughput and global shipping disruptions limited supply. Coal imports rose 20.6 percent YoY to 42.7 Mt ahead of winter stockpiling by utilities. Capacity additions reached 158.7 GW installed year-to-date through June 2026, led by solar at 72.1 GW.
